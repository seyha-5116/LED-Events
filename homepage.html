How We Work

1. Consultation & Briefing

We start by understanding your event objectives, audience, and technical requirements to ensure the right solution.

2. Concept & Proposal

Our team develops a tailored proposal, including event layout, equipment plan, and creative direction.

3. Technical Planning

We finalize all technical details, from LED screen configuration to sound, lighting, and stage setup for optimal performance.

4. Setup & Installation

Our team handles full on-site setup, ensuring all equipment is installed safely, efficiently, and ready before the event.

5. Live Operation & Management

During the event, our technicians manage all systems in real time to ensure smooth, high-quality execution.

6. Dismantling & Evaluation

After the event, we carry out efficient dismantling and review the project to continuously improve our service.
